# Environment Setup

Complete the steps below before starting any lab. You will connect to a Windows virtual machine hosted on IBM TechZone and sign into IBM Bob.

:::info

Your lab instructor will provide your **TechZone reservation link** and **IBMid credentials** before the session begins. If you have not received these, contact your instructor before proceeding.

:::

---

## Step 1 — Find Your Connection Details

Navigate to your TechZone Reservation page and click on your reservation. Under **Published Services**, you will see the connection endpoints assigned to your environment. Each attendee gets unique port numbers.

![TechZone Published Services showing RDP and console endpoints](/img/getting-started/image1.png)

:::tip

Keep this page open — you will refer back to it for credentials and port numbers throughout setup.

:::

---

## Step 2 — Connect to the Virtual Machine

Choose your connection method. **Remote Desktop is strongly recommended** for the best experience.

<Tabs groupId="connection-method">
  <TabItem value="rdp" label="Option 1: Remote Desktop (Recommended)" default>

### Windows

1. Click the **Search bar** at the bottom of your screen and search for **Remote Desktop Connection**.

   ![Searching for Remote Desktop Connection on Windows](/img/getting-started/image2.png)

2. Copy the **Remote Desktop QSE** link from your TechZone reservation page. Remove any `http://` or `https://` prefix, then paste it into the **Computer** field and click **Connect**.

   ![Pasting the RDP link into Remote Desktop Connection](/img/getting-started/image3.png)

3. When the credentials prompt appears, enter the username and password shown in parentheses next to the Remote Desktop QSE link on your reservation page, then click **OK**.

4. If you see a certificate warning, click **Yes** to proceed.

   ![Certificate warning — click Yes to continue](/img/getting-started/image4.png)

---

### Mac

1. If you do not have the **Windows App** installed, open the App Store, search for **Windows App**, and install it.

   ![Installing Windows App from the Mac App Store](/img/getting-started/image5.png)

2. Open the Windows App, click the **+** icon in the upper right, then select **Add PC**.

   ![Adding a new PC in Windows App on Mac](/img/getting-started/image6.png)

3. Copy the **Remote Desktop QSE** link from your TechZone reservation page. Remove any `https://` prefix, paste it into the **PC name** field, then click **Add**.

   ![Entering the PC name in Windows App](/img/getting-started/image7.png)

4. Double-click the PC you just added. When prompted for credentials, enter the username and password shown in parentheses next to the Remote Desktop QSE link on your reservation page. Press **Continue** or **OK** until the remote desktop loads.

   ![Entering credentials in Windows App on Mac](/img/getting-started/image8.png)

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem value="browser" label="Option 2: Browser Connection">

:::warning

Browser-based access is available as a fallback but is **not recommended** — it is slower and lacks clipboard support.

:::

From your TechZone reservation page, click the **Console** button next to the virtual machine you want to access.

![TechZone console button for browser-based VM access](/img/getting-started/image9.png)

:::tip Browser Console Tips

Once inside the browser console, use the toolbar controls shown below:

![Browser console toolbar — CAD, Resize, and Send Text buttons](/img/getting-started/image10.png)

| Button | What it does |
|--------|-------------|
| **CAD (A)** | Sends `Ctrl+Alt+Delete` to the VM |
| **Resize (B)** | Changes the display resolution (e.g. 1024×768) |
| **Send Text (C)** | Pastes text from your local clipboard into the VM |

:::

## Step 3 — Sign Into IBM Bob

IBM Bob is the AI assistant used throughout the labs. Complete the following steps after connecting to the virtual machine.

1. On the Windows VM desktop or taskbar, click the **robot icon** to open IBM Bob.

   ![Robot icon on the VM taskbar for launching IBM Bob](/img/getting-started/image11.png)

2. Click **Log in to Bob**.

   ![IBM Bob login screen — click Log in to Bob](/img/getting-started/image12.png)

3. When browser pop-ups appear asking for permission, click **Allow** and then **Open**.

   ![Browser permission pop-ups — click Allow then Open](/img/getting-started/image13.png)

4. Chrome will open with the IBM Bob authentication page.

   ![IBM Bob authentication page in Chrome](/img/getting-started/image14.png)

5. Enter the **IBMid** credentials provided by your lab instructor and sign in.

   ![Signing into IBM Bob with IBMid credentials](/img/getting-started/image15.png)

---

Once you are signed into IBM Bob, your environment is fully configured. Proceed to the first lab.
