# IBM GCM Quantum Safe Lab Series

Welcome to the IBM Guardium Cryptography Manager (GCM) Quantum Safe hands-on lab series. These labs guide you through the end-to-end journey of discovering, analyzing, and remediating cryptographic risk in preparation for a post-quantum world.

## What You'll Learn

By completing this lab series, you will be able to:

- Discover and inventory all cryptographic assets across your environment using a **Cryptography Bill of Materials (CBOM)**
- Analyze cryptographic risk and enforce quantum-safe policies using **IBM Guardium Cryptography Manager**
- Migrate cryptographic secrets to a managed vault using **HashiCorp Vault**
- Automatically remediate legacy applications with **Quantum Safe Remediator (QSR)**

## Background

The quantum computing era poses an existential threat to today's public-key cryptography. IBM's Quantum Safe portfolio provides an end-to-end framework for organizations to discover, manage, and migrate their cryptographic posture before quantum computers break current encryption standards.
